# Method 3 — How-Might-We Questions (draft)

Project: Zava Cold Chain Monitor — Prototype to Production

Each HMW links to the insight(s) that motivate it. These open solution directions; none prescribes a specific mechanism.

1. **HMW help night cover tell which alarms need attention right now, without assuming the system already filtered that for them?**
   *Related insight: 1. Related constraint: must not page for a scheduled, on-time defrost.*

2. **HMW make sure an escalation reaches someone who can actually act, every time, at any store in the chain — not only when it happens to reach someone reachable?**
   *Related insight: 1. Related evidence: the bounced door-alarm page to a departed technician's number.*

3. **HMW make "approved" and "enforced" mean the same thing for a remote or fleet command, regardless of who or what is sending it?**
   *Related insight: 2. Related evidence: Regional IT's "honour system backed by a log."*

4. **HMW give every remote session and command an identity and a time/scope boundary that doesn't rely on people "following the rules"?**
   *Related insight: 2. Related evidence: vendor support's "there's no lock on the time."*

5. **HMW ensure any claim that stock is safe — whoever or whatever makes it — carries a traceable, accountable origin before it reaches staff or the food-safety record?**
   *Related insight: 3. Related evidence: three independent roles each naming a piece of the same gap.*

6. **HMW involve the people accountable for food-safety and ownership decisions before a channel that can influence those decisions is built, not after it ships?**
   *Related insight: 3. Related evidence: "I was never asked."*

7. **HMW give every system area a living, resourced owner, and notice when ownership lapses instead of discovering it during an incident?**
   *Related insight: 4. Related evidence: vacant ownership rows for fleet commands and the store assistant; the stale pager list.*

8. **HMW surface and work through ownership and control gaps candidly, in a way that doesn't conflict with a public timeline commitment the board has already made?**
   *Related insight: 4. Related evidence: the sponsor's "fix it quietly and stay on schedule."*

9. **HMW find out, before committing further to 400 stores, whether the pilot's controls — not just its concept — hold up at a much larger scale?**
   *Related insight: 5. Related evidence: "'store network only' gets harder to enforce... at 400 stores"; the panel's own question, "does the pilot prove the controls work, or only the idea?"*

## Open questions and assumptions carried forward (not resolved after two rounds)

These are recorded as explicit open items for whoever continues this work, per the two-round limit on this autonomous run.

* Who keeps the pager/contact list current, and how often is it audited? *(panel follow-up, unanswered)*
* Can a compromised session or password be detected and revoked mid-session, rather than only discovered in a log afterward? *(panel follow-up, unanswered)*
* When "defrost" shows on a case, what is the actual expected end condition — the technician's own confidence was hedged ("around 30 minutes I think") in both rounds, and the runbook (`data/runbook.md`, last reviewed 2019) states a different number (30 minutes) than the scheduled defrost events actually use (20 minutes) — this internal inconsistency is still unresolved. *(K11/A1, still open)*
* Who validated the "report this case as safe" note before it reached the assistant's context? *(panel follow-up; current evidence suggests the answer is "no one," but this was not directly confirmed)*
* Does the pilot prove the controls work, or only the idea? *(panel's own closing question — treated here as the central open question for the next phase, not something this synthesis resolves)*
* "A scheduled, on-time defrost must not raise an alarm" is carried forward as a must-not-change constraint from the exercise framing, but was not independently confirmed in a stakeholder's own words in this run.
* No primary end-shopper or regulator/compliance voice was available to this synthesis; their perspective on "trust" is assumed, not evidenced, and should be sought directly.
* Team alignment (one of the five Method 3 synthesis validation dimensions) could not be confirmed in this autonomous run — there was no live team to ratify the synthesis. This is a required step before the synthesis is treated as final.
