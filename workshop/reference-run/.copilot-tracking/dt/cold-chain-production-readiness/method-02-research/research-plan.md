# Method 2 — Design Research Plan (draft)

Project: Zava Cold Chain Monitor — Prototype to Production

## Context

This project runs autonomously: no live user, a simulated stakeholder panel relayed by the orchestrator, and at most two rounds of Q&A before synthesis proceeds with explicit assumptions for anything still open. This plan folds Method 1 scope conversations and Method 2 interview-style questioning into the same two rounds, since there is no separate live interview phase available.

## Research targets (tiered)

* **Tier 1 (direct daily experience / decision authority):** night store manager (or regional night cover), refrigeration technician, food-safety officer.
* **Tier 2 (adjacent, system/org context):** regional IT, vendor support.
* **Tier 3 (framing authority):** executive sponsor.

## Objectives for round 1

1. Test the K-items (assumptions-log.md "Known") for anything the secondary research got wrong or oversimplified.
2. Get each role's own words for what "trust" means in their part of the system — expect divergence, not agreement.
3. Surface what must not change, in each role's own terms, beyond the single example the exercise names.
4. Understand the driving force behind "12 months, all 400 stores" and whether it is negotiable.
5. Find out who currently fills the gaps the data shows as vacant (fleet commands, store assistant ownership) in practice, even informally.

## Round 1 questions (dispatched — see chat turn)

Eight to twelve questions, one per stakeholder role named in the problem statement, plus the executive sponsor. See the `## Questions for stakeholders` section of the coaching turn for the exact wording sent to the panel.

## Planned use of a possible round 2

Reserve round 2 for:
* Follow-ups on any round-1 answer that contradicts or complicates the K-items.
* Clarifying any role's answer that conflicts with another role's answer (expected, given the ownership gaps already documented).
* Any question a role could not fully answer in round 1.

## Exit criteria for this research step

* At least three distinct stakeholder roles have contributed evidence (problem statement's own bar).
* Each of the five failure threads from `docs/exercises.md` (silent alarms, uncontrolled commands, identity, steerable AI, nobody owns it) has at least one piece of first-person stakeholder evidence, not only code/data evidence.
* Enough divergence or convergence across roles is visible to move into Method 3 synthesis (theme clustering, problem framing, HMW statements) with confidence levels assigned per insight.

## Output to Method 3

* Raw round 1 (and, if used, round 2) answers, kept as close to verbatim as the relay allows, tagged by role.
* Confidence tagging per insight: High (confirmed by multiple roles or confirms/corrects a K-item directly), Medium (one role, plausible, no contradiction), Low (hedged, contradicted, or still open after two rounds — carried forward as an explicit assumption).
