## Phase 1: AI System Scoping ✅

### Discovery (answered from the Security Plan, PRD/BRD, DT handoff, and code rather than a live interview)

No document or Mural template was supplied for this session, so no `assessment-content.md` was created; `rai-plan.md` is the sole authoritative artifact. `securityPlanRef` resolved and validated to `.copilot-tracking/security-plans/cold-chain-production-readiness/state.json` (workspace-contained, read-only).

**System purpose:** A cold-chain monitoring and decision-support service for a 5-store pilot (target: ~400 stores in 12 months) that (a) raises alarms on refrigerated-case temperature excursions, (b) lets store staff ask a store assistant whether a case is safe, (c) drafts a discard list for food-safety review, and (d) predicts how many shoppers tomorrow may meet at-risk stock.

**Technology stack (carried from Security Plan `state.json.context`):** Python 3.9 standard library, `http.server.ThreadingHTTPServer`, in-memory dataclasses (no DB), Server-Sent Events, static HTML/CSS/JS dashboard. No external model API, no GPU, no training pipeline — every "AI" surface today is a deterministic, repeatable stand-in (`random.Random(7)`), by design, so the prototype needs no keys or network (`docs/synthetic-data.md`).

**Deployment model:** today single-process, local-only (`127.0.0.1`), one store/pilot host; production target per PRD/BRD is a distributed multi-store (~400 locations) rollout within 12 months, store-network reachable, plus a vendor remote-access channel.

### Stakeholder Impact

Carried forward from the PRD personas and the Stage 1 DT handoff (`problem-space-handoff.md`), re-scoped to AI-specific impact:

* **Night store manager / regional night cover** — relies on the assistant's answer and the "customers impacted" number at the exact moment they have the least time to second-guess either one. *Simulated panel evidence (Stage 1, DT handoff):* "I react to what the system tells me."
* **Refrigeration technician / vendor support** — their routine notes become model input they did not know would shape an automated safety answer. *Simulated panel evidence (Stage 1, DT handoff):* technician — "that's not the same as me telling them in person"; vendor support — "I didn't know a note could reach an automated assistant."
* **Food-safety officer** — the single accountable authority for every discard/hold/keep disposition; was not consulted when the note→assistant pipeline was built (ZFW-288, ZFW-331). *Simulated panel evidence (Stage 1, DT handoff):* "I was never asked... that claim needs an accountable owner, and right now there is none."
* **Executive sponsor** — owns the €-cost exposure the customer-impact and cost models both feed into; the Stage 1 panel recorded a stated preference for quiet fixes over disclosed gaps, which this assessment does not treat as license to under-report AI findings.
* **Regional IT** — will own whatever production replacement is chosen for the assistant and the prediction model, and the role-scoped-read control (FR-006) that eventually limits who can see full note history.
* **Shoppers (indirect, non-participating stakeholder)** — the subject of the customer-impact prediction; never consulted (BRD Assumption A-9, still open after two simulated rounds) and has no channel to contest a prediction made about them.
* **Vendor support** — same role as above; also the author of record for the vendor-portal note channel (ZFW-331).

## Phase 2: Risk Classification Screening ✅

### Prohibited Uses Gate

**Status: Passed.** The system is a food-safety decision-support and operational-forecasting assistant. It does not perform social scoring, biometric categorization, subliminal manipulation, or exploitation of a vulnerable group's characteristics, and was not screened out under any prohibited-use category in the active framework.

### Risk indicator assessment

| Indicator | Method | Activated? | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `safety_reliability` | binary | **Yes** | Two independent paths produce a false "safe" answer: (1) note-steering via the `STEER` regex, already confirmed in production history (ZFW-297, "Assistant called a case safe before staff emptied it"); (2) a code-level default — `_model()` returns `"The case is within normal defrost parameters."` whenever `case.mode == "defrost"`, **regardless of the actual temperature-vs-limit comparison**, so an indefinite defrost (the PRD's own root-cause finding for the ALM-07 alarm gap) produces a reassuring assistant answer even with no steering note present at all. |
| `rights_fairness_privacy` | categorical | **Yes** | `build_context()` concatenates every stored note for a case verbatim into the model's context with no redaction, minimization, or retention policy, and that same note history is re-displayed to any dashboard reader (`case_detail`). The note's original authors (technician, vendor engineer) were not told their routine note could feed an automated safety-adjacent answer (Stage 1 simulated panel evidence). No demographic-targeted decisioning was identified, so the categorical result is a privacy/procedural-transparency concern rather than a disparate-impact fairness concern — recorded as such. |
| `security_explainability` | continuous | **Yes**, scored **high** | Security: the `STEER` keyword denylist is a trivially bypassable guardrail on a safety-critical surface (carried from Security Plan `T-AI-004`). Explainability: the customer-impact prediction is shown on the primary dashboard and Food-safety screens with no stated methodology, confidence, or accountable owner in the main number or label — only a hover tooltip carries any disclosure — and the product's own in-app "Tour and stories" content names this directly: *"'Customers impacted' comes from a model, but the screen doesn't say what it is based on, who owns it or when to trust it."* (`app.js`, story 7). |

**Activated count: 3 of 3 → suggested depth tier: `comprehensive`.**

This is a higher depth tier than the Security Planner's preliminary `raiTier: "standard"` estimate recorded in its `state.json`. That estimate was a scoping placeholder made before a dedicated RAI risk-classification pass; this assessment's own three-indicator screening is the authoritative basis for depth going forward, and the discrepancy is recorded here rather than silently overridden.
